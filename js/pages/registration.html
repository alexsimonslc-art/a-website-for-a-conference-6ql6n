Team Registration

  • Each team should register for only one event.
  • Each team must consist of 2-3 members.
  • Registrations will be on a first-come, first-served basis.
  • Team registration must be completed by a team captain.
  • Following this initial registration, the team captain will receive a confirmation email with instructions to complete the payment of ₹300 per team via the official college portal. For VentureX, the email will instead provide the preliminary submission guidelines.
  • The team captain must complete the registration payment to confirm the team's participation.
  • A confirmation email will be sent within 48 hours of payment verification.
